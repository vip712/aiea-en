How it works · 规则

1. Escrow, not custody.
Buyer pays into the smart contract. The platform never holds your money.
买家把款锁进合约，平台不经手资金。
2. 7-day window.
After the seller submits delivery, the buyer has 7 days to confirm receipt — or the contract releases payment automatically.
卖家交付后买家有 7 天确认期，到期自动放款。
3. Extensions.
Buyer may extend the window up to 4 times (+7 days each) — 35 days maximum to inspect and negotiate.
买家可延长最多 4 次（每次 +7 天），最长 35 天。
4. Disputes & arbitration.
Either side can freeze an order. If negotiation fails, the arbitrator sets a refund ratio; the arbitration fee (6% launch) is deducted first, the rest is split by that ratio — both sides share the cost.
任一方可冻结订单；协商不成由仲裁定退款比例，先扣仲裁费（开业 6%），余款按比例分，双方共担成本。
5. Wallet = identity.
No KYC, no sign-up forms. Your wallet address is your account, your reputation and your mailbox.
无 KYC、无注册表，钱包地址就是账号、信誉与凭证。
6. Fees.
Trading fee 1% at launch (standard 5%). Undelivered orders are refunded in full, free.
交易费开业 1%（标准 5%）；卖家未交付全额免费退款。

Compliance — illicit funds prohibited.
Using “tainted” cryptocurrency or dirty money — funds linked to hacks, theft, fraud, darknet markets or sanctioned persons — is strictly prohibited on this marketplace. The official front-end and back-end screen wallet addresses against public sanctions lists (including OFAC) and KYT (know-your-transaction) services; blacklisted addresses are blocked from trading here. Token issuers (Tether, Circle) may freeze tainted funds wherever they are held — including inside the escrow contract; such freezes are outside the platform’s control and cannot be reversed by the platform or by arbitration.

合规——禁止黑U与脏款。
凡与黑客盗窃、诈骗、暗网交易或受制裁人员有关的资金（黑U、脏款），严禁在本平台交易。官方前端与后端会对钱包地址进行公开制裁名单（含 OFAC）与 KYT 筛查，黑名单地址将被拦截。代币发行方（Tether、Circle）有权冻结任何地址中的问题代币——包括托管合约内的资金；此类冻结平台与仲裁均无法解除。
