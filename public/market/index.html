How it works · 规则

1. Escrow, not custody.
Buyer pays into the smart contract. The platform never holds your money.
买家把款锁进合约，平台不经手资金。
2. 7-day window.
After the seller submits delivery, the buyer has 7 days to confirm receipt — or the contract releases payment automatically.
卖家交付后买家有 7 天确认期，到期自动放款。
3. Extensions.
Buyer may extend the window up to 4 times (+7 days each) — 35 days maximum to inspect and negotiate.
买家可延长最多 4 次（每次 +7 天），最长 35 天。
4. Disputes & arbitration.
Either side can freeze an order. If negotiation fails, the arbitrator sets a refund ratio; the arbitration fee (6% launch) is deducted first, the rest is split by that ratio — both sides share the cost.
任一方可冻结订单；协商不成由仲裁定退款比例，先扣仲裁费（开业 6%），余款按比例分，双方共担成本。
5. Wallet = identity.
No KYC, no sign-up forms. Your wallet address is your account, your reputation and your mailbox.
无 KYC、无注册表，钱包地址就是账号、信誉与凭证。
6. Fees.
Trading fee 1% at launch (standard 5%). Undelivered orders are refunded in full, free.
交易费开业 1%（标准 5%）；卖家未交付全额免费退款。
